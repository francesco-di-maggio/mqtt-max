mqtt·max

MIDI over MQTT, for Max.

Exchange MIDI and data between Max and any device that speaks MQTT.

Open the phone appTilt, XY pad and slider as MIDI. Runs in the browser, no install. →

How it works

  1. 01 DevicePublishes to an MQTT broker
  2. 02 BrokerRelays it over any network
  3. 03 MaxPlays it as MIDI, or as data
